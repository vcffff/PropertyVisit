# PropertyVisit

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Buyers browsing properties and booking viewings are the primary audience. Managers maintain properties and manage viewing requests.

## Product Purpose

Help buyers find a property and book a viewing, keeping manager workflows practical.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing Flutter application with authentication, property catalog and details, viewing booking, buyer viewing history, and manager screens. Preserve existing functionality and Russian copy. Prioritize iPhone and Android phones, with appropriate native behavior and sensible larger-window layouts.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the PropertyVisit name. The user explicitly requests replacement of the current visual design with a usable, deliberately designed interface rather than generic AI styling.

## Evidence on Hand

Implementation under `propertyvisit/lib/`; sample inventory in `propertyvisit/lib/core/data/mock_store.dart`. Demo accounts are labeled in the login screen. Do not invent market claims, testimonials, or capabilities.

## Product Principles

- Make property comparison and viewing booking the primary tasks.
- Keep property facts and booking status easy to scan.
- Preserve buyer and manager workflows.
- Respect phone ergonomics and native expectations.

## Open Decisions

Specific accessibility requirements and deployment targets beyond iPhone and Android are not established.
